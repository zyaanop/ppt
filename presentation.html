MedJar

A multi-agent consensus system where specialist LLM agents debate a patient's case, ground their reasoning in medical literature, and converge on a single diagnostic report.

🩻 Radiologist🫀 Cardiologist🎗️ Oncologist🧠 + Extensible

Medical Judgment via Agent Reasoning — assistive · human-in-the-loop · not autonomous

The Problem

Complex diagnosis fails for structural reasons

≈ 1 in 20

adults experience a diagnostic error in outpatient care — concentrated in complex, multi-system cases.

Specialty silos

A cardiologist and an oncologist rarely reason on the same case at the same time.

Cognitive bias

Anchoring and premature closure shut down the differential too early.

Stale knowledge + hallucination

Guidelines evolve faster than anyone can track — and a lone LLM asserts false facts fluently.

A single "expert" model collapses the field's diversity into one distribution. MedJar restores the productive tension between specialists.

Core Insight
No single mind holds the whole of medicine. The best diagnoses emerge from a room of disagreeing experts forced to reconcile their evidence.

Debate > Vote

Use the reasons, not just the answers.

Disagreement = Signal

Conflict drives retrieval & escalation.

Ground everything

No claim without a citation.

Distinct reasoning priors

Personas encode what this specialist worries about first — preserving the diversity that makes debate valuable.

Scoped retrieval

Each agent preferentially retrieves from its own corpus (atlases, ESC/ACC, staging manuals) — global retrieval would homogenize views.

Plug-in registry → add Neurology, Infectious Disease, Pathology with only a persona + scope.

The Debate Protocol · MedJar's core

Propose → Critique → Rebut → Converge

1 · Propose

No cross-talk. Independent differentials prevent anchoring & preserve diversity.

2 · Critique

Anonymized & adversarial. Attack weak support, misread evidence, missing tests.

3 · Rebut

Defend, concede, or revise. Disagreement triggers targeted retrieval.

4 · Converge

Measure agreement → aggregate, loop again, or escalate to a human.

Why not majority vote?

Voting discards why agents disagree and silences the minority "can't-miss" diagnosis. Debate keeps the reasons.

Red-flag override

Any agent raising a can't-miss dx (dissection, PE, sepsis, malignancy) above threshold forces immediate escalation.

Consensus Mathematics

Confidence-weighted, evidence-adjusted aggregation

Ensemble score S(h)

S(h) = Σᵢ wᵢ(h)·cᵢ·pᵢ(h) ⁄ Σᵢ wᵢ(h)·cᵢ

wᵢ(h) soft specialty weight · cᵢ calibrated confidence · pᵢ(h) agent likelihood.

Evidence adjustment

S*(h) = σ( α·logit S(h) + β·E(h) )

Rewards higher-grade, more recent grounded evidence.

Disagreement D̄

Disagree(h) = Σᵢ wᵢcᵢ(pᵢ(h)−S(h))² ⁄ Σᵢ wᵢcᵢ

Weighted variance across agents → the control signal.

Stopping rule

  • converge D̄ ≤ τ_agree
  • bound round r = R_max
  • escalate red-flag > τ_flag

Worked Case · synthetic & illustrative

58 y/o · dyspnea · weight loss · spiculated RUL nodule · ↑troponin · pericardial effusion

🩻 Radiologist

Spiculated RUL nodule → primary lung malignancy high; recommends PET-CT + biopsy; notes effusion.

🫀 Cardiologist

↑troponin + effusion → must exclude ACS & tamponade; troponin may be demand ischemia.

🎗️ Oncologist

Suspects primary lung Ca; effusion could be malignant → upstage… but needs cytology.

Debate resolves

Consensus: tissue + effusion cytology are the discriminating tests. Disagreement on stage remains.

Red-flag → escalate

Tamponade is the can't-miss. Report ships with an explicit open question: hemodynamic compromise? → urgent human review.

Single vs. ensemble · vote vs. debate · with/without RAG · calibrated vs. raw confidence.

Human eval + silent trial

Blinded specialists rate correctness/safety/actionability; prospective read-only comparison to confirmed diagnoses.

Primary safety endpoint = false-reassurance rate (down-ranking a can't-miss dx that was in fact present).

Safety · Ethics · Regulatory

Assistive by design — the clinician decides

Human gate

Mandatory sign-off before any recommendation informs care. Uncertainty & red-flags escalate rather than closing the case.

Privacy & security

HIPAA/GDPR de-id · PHI enclave · encryption · full access audit · no training on patient data without governance.

Regulatory posture

Likely SaMD (FDA) / MDR (EU). Transparent evidence targets the "clinician can independently review the basis" pathway. Follows GMLP.

Bias & equity

Corpus + evaluation stratified by demographics; calibration checked per subgroup; continuous fairness monitoring.

⚠ MedJar is decision-support. It does not autonomously diagnose or treat, and is not a substitute for professional medical judgment.
